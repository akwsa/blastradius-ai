# Privacy Policy for BlastRadius AI

Last updated: November 2026

BlastRadius AI is built natively on Atlassian Forge. 
1. **Zero Egress Data:** All analysis, compute, and Jira/Confluence context evaluation run 100% inside Atlassian Cloud isolated Forge containers. No data is ever transmitted to external servers or third-party databases.
2. **Data Retention:** Risk scores and audit metadata are stored exclusively within the customer's own Atlassian Cloud site via native Forge Storage.
3. **Contact:** For support or privacy questions, contact wkagung@gmail.com.
